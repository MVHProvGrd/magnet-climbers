Pickups 8

Floating power-ups. Collecting one is instant and applies to that climber only. Timed effects stack up to a cap. game.ts applyPickup · CFG.effectDurations
  • Super Magnet 8s, stacks to 16s — glass, plastic, paper and plates all take a grip, and neither attacker can touch you.
  • Kitchen Timer 6s, stacks to 12s. Reach Badge 14s, stacks to 28s.
  • Candy Drop 9s, stacks to 18s — the red line drops to 30% speed while it runs.
  • Chill mode pays no coins or gems and unlocks nothing.

Gadgets 35

Two to a door, on every fourth door, cycling swing → rotor → clip → polarity as you climb. Only the silver part is ever a hold: ride it, then fling. Each photographed assembly hangs or turns about its own measured point, never a shared one. gadgets.ts · gadget-pivots.ts
  • Swing — pendulum, ±0.5 rad on a 45px arm. Hangs still until something touches it.
  • Clip — gentler pendulum, ±0.22 rad. The clip grips; the paper under it does not.
  • Rotor — turns continuously at 0.95 rad/s, the grip orbiting 20px off the spindle.
  • Polarity — a 6s cycle: blue holds for 3s, then red pushes for 3s, counting down before each flip.
  • A doodle is a bit of paper, so it is only ever clipped — never hung off a keyring chain.

Souvenir plates 22

Magnetic field plates. S pulls an airborne climber in and catches them; N pushes away, and the wider the arcs the stronger it is — the big ones are slingshots. Your aim dots turn blue where one bends the flight. The souvenir photograph is purely cosmetic: polarity, timing and collision are identical across all fourteen, and which one you see is a hash of the gadget's id. gadgets.ts polarityDestination

Surfaces 9

What the door is made of. Magnets catch on bare steel — and on paper, which is pinned up by its own magnet, so yours catches on it too. world.ts isMetal · items.ts grips
  • glass and trim — no magnetic hold at all. Cross in flight, or go round by the steel edges.
  • void — nothing there to touch. The handle is the exception: a metal island laid across the slippery bits.
  • sticker — paper and the calendar. Climb them exactly like the door.

Paper 32

Drawings, notes, prints and photos, each pinned under its own magnet — so they hold you. Anti-repeat remembers the last ten, so the same note should not surface twice in a stretch. world.ts recentPapers

Advertising magnets 16

Printed vinyl magnets that slide along sideways, vertical or zigzag paths. Contact knocks a climber loose with a 420px/s kick and costs a heart, then a moment of invulnerability. game.ts bumpers · CFG.bumperKnock

Toy magnets 13

Toys on the door. Unlike the advertising magnets these never slide and never cost a heart, and neither form is a hold. Stuck straight on by its magnet backing a toy carries a small field — and being a magnet it sits either way round, so from v19 it is as likely to pull you in as push you off. The arcs say which: red creeping outward pushes, blue closing inward pulls. world.ts v19 toys

Toy keyrings 13

The same thirteen toys again, hung off a magnetic hook on a short chain. The whole thing swings when grabbed and only the silver hook and chain grip — the toy on the end never does. None has a photograph of its own: each is the toy composited onto the keychain hardware at the hook point measured on that toy, so the banana hangs from its stem and the letter block from its top corner. These pictures are rendered by the game's own draw code. items.ts TOY_HOOKS · gadget-art.ts

Hazards 2

Two attackers. Both warn before they land, both knock you loose and cost a heart, and neither can touch a climber carrying a Super Magnet. game.ts stepHand · cat-paw.ts
  • Cooper's Hand — 1.1s warning, then a curved sweep. First at 18s, then every 22s, closing to 9s the higher you climb.
  • The Cat's Paw — 0.75s warning ring, then three taps, the second one deepest.
  • Both at once on 7% of attacks.

Variants and parts 14

Files the build loads that are not elements in their own right. The stocked door panels are picked by position, not by item, so one glass surface can come up as any of these six; the charm sheets and the compass needle are composited into gadgets at draw time; the claw marks are decals the cat leaves behind. obstacle-art.ts OBSTACLE_IDS · gadget-art.ts

Outside the run 9
