Privacy Policy

Last updated September 14, 2026

Magnet Climbers is a free browser game. It has no accounts, no email sign-up, no ads and no tracking pixels. This page explains the little it does keep.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, creatures, best heights, settings) is saved in your browser's local storage. A random player id such as p-k3f9… and a secret token are generated the first time you play; they identify your save and nothing else. Nothing on your device is read besides that save.

What is sent to our servers

  • Scores. Your display name, best heights and total metres climbed, tied to your random player id, so the scoreboards work.
  • Cloud save. A copy of your progress so it can follow you to another device via a link code. It is readable only with your token.
  • Chat. Messages you post in the global chat, with your display name and player id. The chat is public to everyone playing. Messages are filtered for profanity and links, kept for a short while, and can be removed by us at any time.
  • Share cards. When you share a challenge link, the height and name in it are checked against your scoreboard entry to draw the card.

We do not collect your email, real name, location, contacts or anything from your device beyond the above. Display names are chosen by you; please do not use your full real name.

Where it lives

The game is served by GitHub Pages and the scoreboard, cloud save and chat run on Cloudflare Workers and D1. Those providers keep standard server logs (IP address, browser type, time of request) for security and operations under their own policies: GitHub, Cloudflare. We do not use analytics services.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and asks for no personal information. The global chat is public: do not post your name, school, phone number, address or links. If you are a parent and want a child's messages or scores removed, email us with the display name shown on the scoreboard.

Cookies

None. Local storage holds your save only. Clearing site data in your browser deletes it (use a link code from Settings first if you want to keep it).

Deleting your data

Email admin@magnetclimbers.com with your display name or player id (Settings shows the first characters of it). We will remove your scores, cloud save and chat messages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top moves and the game's update banner will mention it.
